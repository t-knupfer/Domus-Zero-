Our Process

Turn Your Property Sustainable in Weeks

  • 01

    Contact Us

    Contact us by phone, email or through the website. We will ask for some basic information about your property, such as recent energy bills, the address, photos of the building and details of your existing energy systems.

  • 02

    Schedule Initial Zoom/MS Teams Video Call

    We will ask you to share the information we requested under point 01.

  • 03

    Remote Feasibility Analysis

    We will do remote feasibility analyses including location, Comune, space, and other characteristics of your property.

  • 04

    First Site Visit

    We will schedule with you a 3h site visit to collect all required data (ideally while you are present).

  • 05

    Tailored Solution

    We develop a tailored and detailed solution including a specific offer. If you accept the offer, we will inform you within a few days about the detailed implementation plan/schedule.

  • 06

    High-Quality Installation

    We install the integrated solar PV and heat pump system with a focus on quality, reliability and minimal disruption to your property.

  • 07

    Hand Over

    Final turnkey hand over of property-specific sustainable solution.

  • 08

    Support

    Post-realization support as needed and requested by you.
