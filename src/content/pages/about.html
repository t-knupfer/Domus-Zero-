About Domus Zero

Based in Tuscany

Local Expertise in Sustainable Energy

Domus Zero helps home- and agriturismo-owners transition to efficient, integrated solar PV and heat pump systems tailored to their properties.

Based in Tuscany, we work with clients across the region, including Chianti, Greve, Impruneta and Siena, and across Italy, combining local knowledge with a reliable, carefully managed approach to sustainable energy projects.

Our experience spans hundreds of successfully completed sustainable energy projects. We have used that experience to refine how solar and heat pump projects are planned and installed, with a focus on reliability, quality and a positive business case for our clients.
