Our Turnkey Process

Our Process

Turn Your Home or Agriturismo Sustainable in Weeks

  • 01

    Contact Us

    Contact us by phone, email or through the website. We will ask for some basic information about your property, such as recent energy bills, the address, photos of the building and details of your existing energy systems.

  • 02

    Initial Video Consultation

    We will review the information you provide and discuss your property, current energy system, objectives and any initial questions about solar PV, heat pumps or the installation process.

  • 03

    Remote Feasibility Analysis

    We carry out an initial feasibility analysis based on the location, Comune, available space, existing energy systems and other relevant characteristics of your property.

  • 04

    First Site Visit

    We schedule an on-site visit of three hours to collect the technical information required to design the appropriate solar PV and heat pump solution for your property.

  • 05

    Tailored Solution & Fixed-Price Proposal

    We develop a detailed solution tailored to your property and provide a clear, fixed-price proposal. Once you accept the offer, we prepare the detailed implementation plan and installation schedule.

  • 06

    High-Quality Installation

    We install the integrated solar PV and heat pump system with a focus on quality, reliability and minimal disruption to your home or agriturismo.

  • 07

    Turnkey Handover

    Once installation and final checks are complete, we hand over the finished system as a fully implemented turnkey solution tailored to your property.

  • 08

    Support

    Post-realization support as needed and requested by you.
